# Bounded Synthetic Content

Match synthetic settings, manuals, indexes, or interfaces to the apparent probe target without claims of certain intent.
Keep names, timestamps, and links coherent throughout the approved lifetime.
Exclude real data, personal information, operational commands, private trap flags, risk scores, seeds, and policy fields.
Keep visible navigation outside normal workflows and sitemaps.
For hidden elements, apply [event rules](detection-and-response.md).

Use operator-controlled infrastructure and internal server-side dispatch with bounded asset validation.
Do not accept arbitrary destination URLs or send traffic, credentials, or evidence externally.
For necessary redirects, enforce an exact local allowlist after canonical parsing and decoding.
Keep synthetic sessions, forms, caches, and handlers separate from real account state.
Never execute submitted commands or verify credentials against real providers.
Do not create parser bombs, browser loops, malicious payloads, or remote-agent instruction overrides.

Apply [budgets](operating-costs.md) to actual admitted work, not just potential graph size.
Generate only requested content, not an unlimited graph.
Measure actual follow-up requests, nodes, depth, and repeats separately from responses.
Compare traversal with bytes, handler time, connections, retained state, and normal-traffic impact.
Test coherence, redirect safety, isolation, bounded traversal, budget exhaustion, and emergency disable.
Do not infer remote computation or token consumption from requests.
